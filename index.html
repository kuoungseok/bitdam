Product Spec

The current public spec frames Tiger Studio as a video editor with creator-focused workbenches around the timeline.

Timeline Video Editor

Preview, timeline, media pool, inspector, effects, transitions, typography, export, and render queue.

Screen Recording Polish

Capture, cursor sidecars, zoom planning, click rings, hotkey badges, and polished screen-video export.

Character Actors

Live2D, Spine, MMD, and VRM workflows treated as editable actor surfaces inside video projects.

Voice Lab and Subtitles

Subtitle-to-voice direction, actor lip-sync hooks, and sidecar-based voice generation boundaries.

Music Lab and Audio

MIDI-first Music Lab, Sound Editor, loudness helpers, mix controls, and audio workflow diagnostics.

PPT / Presentation Pages

Timeline-native presentation authoring for slides, media, typography, and video export paths.

AR / PBR / 3D Compositing

3D object preview, depth-aware placement, material controls, shadow/reflection targets, and render parity checks.

AI Actions and Automation

Structured action surfaces for reviewable edits, QA, catalog evidence, and local automation.

Positioning

Tiger Studio is not trying to replace DaVinci Resolve, CapCut, OBS, Live2D Cubism, or VTube Studio directly. It brings the creator-facing parts together into one local editing workflow.

Resolve급 전문 후반작업 툴이나 Cubism 같은 모델 제작툴을 대체하는 것이 아니라, 캐릭터와 화면녹화, 쇼츠, 음성, 음악을 하나의 영상편집 흐름으로 묶는 것이 핵심입니다.
